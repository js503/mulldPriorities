See what your colony needs. Give your pawns useful work. Review before applying.

mulld Priorities brings colony needs and work rules into RimWorld’s Work tab.

• Track food runway, care coverage and harvest pressure.
• Assign useful primary work, backups and spare-time jobs.
• Choose Balanced, Clean, Harvest or Haul—or save your own preset.
• Preview proposed numbers in the work grid before applying.
• Keep control with exclusions, locks, discard and undo.

Manual approval by default. Daily automation and performance diagnostics are optional and off by default.

Requires RimWorld 1.7 and Harmony. Uses standard Off / 1–4 priorities.

Friends-only testing build. Feedback welcome.
