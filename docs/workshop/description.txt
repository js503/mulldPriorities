[b]Colony needs. Useful work. Your approval.[/b]

[h2]01 // Features[/h2]

[b]Read the colony[/b]
Food, care and harvest indicators show where attention is needed.
[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/js503/mulldPriorities/7de84aed47a844c8625e5ef5cb2acdafa4729b1b/docs/workshop/images/needs.png[/img]

[b]Set the rules[/b]
Choose Balanced, Clean, Harvest or Haul. Set primary workers and backups in the compact rules table. Use Edit for advanced options or Save Preset to keep your own setup.
[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/js503/mulldPriorities/7de84aed47a844c8625e5ef5cb2acdafa4729b1b/docs/workshop/images/rules.png[/img]

[b]Approve the work[/b]
Click [b]Preview Changes[/b]. Gold cells show proposed priorities. Nothing changes until you click [b]Apply[/b].
[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/js503/mulldPriorities/7de84aed47a844c8625e5ef5cb2acdafa4729b1b/docs/workshop/images/preview.png[/img]

[b]Your assignments stay yours.[/b]
Exclude colonists, lock jobs, discard a draft or undo the last apply. Daily automation and diagnostics are optional and off by default.

[h2]02 // How to open[/h2]
[list]
[*]Subscribe and enable Harmony and mulld Priorities in Mods, then restart RimWorld.
[*]Load a colony and open the [b]Work[/b] tab.
[*]Click [b]mulld Priorities[/b] above the work grid to expand the panel.
[/list]
